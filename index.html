Rolex-Recipe

Description

A Rolexis a popular street food from Uganda. The name comes from "rolled eggs" and not the luxury watch

It's essentially a chapati rolled around a seasoned egg and vegetable filling, similar to a burrito or wrap

Ingridients

  • 1 chapati, store bought or homemade
  • Egg filling, 2 large eggs
  • Vegetables finely chopped

    • 1/4 small onions

    1 small totmato

    1/4 small carrot

    1/4 cup cabbage

    1/2 small bell pepper optional

  • Seasonings

    • Salt

    Black pepper

    1 Maggi cube

  • Cooking Oil
  • Optional additions

    • Fresh green onoions

    Cheese

    Avocado

Steps for making the Meal

  1. Prepare the Vegetables

    2. Chop onions, tomatos, cabbages,and carrot

  2. Beat eggs

    3. Crack 2 eggs into a bowl, add salt, pepper, and crushed Maggi cubes this is optional and then whisk

  3. Cook filling

    4. Heat 1 table spoon of oil in a pan, Saute onions and veggies for 1 minute. Pour in eggs and scramble until fully cooked

  4. Warm chapati

    5. Heat chapati in a dry pan for 10-15 seconds per side

  5. Assemble

    6. Lay chapati flat. Spoon egg filling in a line down the center

  6. Roll

    7. Fold one side over filling, tuck slightly, then roll tightly like a burrito

  7. Serve

    8. Wrap in foil or paper to hold shape. Ear hot

Chapati-Recipe

Chapati-Recipe

Description

A Chapati is an unleavened flatbread originating from the Indian subcontinent. It's typically round and flat, about 6-8 inches in diameter, with a warm, golden-brown surface marked by darker toasted spots from cooking. The texture is soft and paliable, often with a slight chew, and it can be pulled apart into thin layers when freshly made. It's flavor is mild, nutty, and slightly earthy, made simply from whole flour, water, and salt. Chapatis are commonly cooked on a dry tava or griddle and then briefly puffed over an open flame, giving them a lightly charred, smoky aroma. Served hot, they are used to scoop up curries, vegetables, or lentils, acting as both a utensil and a satisfying, wholesome staple

Ingridients

  • Whole wheat flour

    • The primary ingredient, giving chapatis its characteristic earthly, nutty flavor

  • Water

    • To bind the flour into a soft, pliable dough

  • Salt

    • Added for taste though some regional variations may omit

Steps for making the Meal

  1. Mix whole wheat flour and salt
  2. Gradually add water and knead into a soft, smooth dough
  3. Rest the dough for 15-30 minutes
  4. Divide into small balls
  5. Roll each ball into a thin, round circle
  6. Cook on a hot tava until bubbles form
  7. Flip and cook the other side
  8. Serve hot

Description

Lasagna is a classic Italian casserole dish featuring alternating layers of wide, flat pasta sheets, savory sauces (typically ragù and béchamel), cheese (such as mozzarella, ricotta, or parmesan), and sometimes meat or vegetables. It is baked al forno (in the oven) until bubbly and golden, offering a rich, comforting, and hearty texture

Ingridients

  • Lasagna Sheets: Dried, fresh, or no-boil pasta noodles.
  • Meat / Protein: Ground beef, Italian sausage, or pork.
  • Red Sauce: Marinara sauce, tomato paste, and crushed tomatoes.
  • Aromatics: Diced onions, minced garlic, and olive oil.
  • Herbs / Spices: Fresh basil, dried oregano, salt, and black pepper.
  • Cheese Blend: Ricotta (or cottage cheese), shredded mozzarella, and parmesan.
  • Egg: One whole egg mixed into the ricotta to bind it.

Steps for making the Lasagna

  1. Make the Meat SauceBrown:

    2. Cook meat, onions, and garlic in olive oil until browned.Simmer: Stir in tomatoes, tomato paste, herbs, salt, and pepper.Thicken: Simmer on low heat for 20 minutes until thick

  2. Prepare the Cheese MixtureCombine:

    3. Mix ricotta, half the parmesan, and the egg in a bowl.Season: Add a pinch of salt and pepper, then stir until smooth.

  3. Cook the PastaBoil:

    4. Cook lasagna sheets in salted water per package instructions.Drain: Drain and lay sheets flat on parchment paper to prevent sticking.

  4. Assemble the LayersBase:

    5. Spread a thin layer of meat sauce in a baking dish.Build: Layer pasta sheets, ricotta mixture, meat sauce, and mozzarella.Repeat: Repeat these layers until all ingredients are used.Top: Finish with a final layer of pasta, remaining meat sauce, mozzarella, and parmesan.

  5. Bake and ServeCover:

    6. Cover the dish loosely with foil.Bake: Bake at 190°C (375°F) for 25 minutes.Brown: Remove foil and bake 15 minutes more until bubbly.Rest: Let sit for 15 minutes before slicing so it holds its shape.
